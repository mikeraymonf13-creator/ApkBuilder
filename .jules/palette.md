## 2026-10-11 - Vanilla JS Tab & Form ARIA Accessibility
**Learning:** Custom tab navigation and async form submit buttons in plain HTML/JS UI require explicit `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `role="tabpanel"`, dynamically toggling `aria-selected`, and `aria-live="polite"` status regions for screen reader announcements.
**Action:** Always include ARIA tab semantics, focus-visible styles, and async loading/disabled button states on vanilla JS components.
